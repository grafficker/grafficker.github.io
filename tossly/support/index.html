Tossly Support

Tossly is a swipe-to-clean photo app for iPhone: swipe left to trash, swipe right to keep, review the pile and delete in one confirmed batch.

Need help?

Email grafficker2@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version. We usually reply within two business days.

Frequently asked

I swiped left, is the photo gone?
No. Swiped photos wait in Tossly's trash until you tap Delete and confirm in the iOS dialog. Afterwards they sit in the Photos app's "Recently Deleted" album for up to 30 days.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Tossly. Cancelling keeps Pro until the end of the paid period.

How do I restore a purchase on a new phone?
Open the Pro screen in Tossly and tap "Restore purchases" while signed in with the same Apple ID.

Where do my photos go?
Nowhere. Tossly works entirely on your device and has no server. See the Privacy Policy.
